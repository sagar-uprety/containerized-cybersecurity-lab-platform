A file server used by several teams for backups and shared documents has
been flagged during a routine internal network scan. The server is
reachable on the lab network and may be exposing data without proper
access controls.

Your mission:

1. Use the browser terminal to inspect the lab network.
2. Identify the file-sharing service and the shares it exposes.
3. Determine whether the service requires credentials before granting access.
4. Demonstrate the impact of the exposure using only the seeded demo data.
5. Harden the service so only authorized users can read the share and
   connections are encrypted.
6. Verify authorized access still works after your changes.
7. Run the portal check and record the result.
8. Complete the mandatory feedback form after ending the lab.

- ~/SITREP.txt
